Methods and limitations

Analysis methods

The display compares a rectangular window with a periodic Hann window on the same samples. Amplitudes are corrected for each window’s coherent gain. DC and Nyquist bins are not doubled.

This is a one-sided amplitude spectrum, not a power spectral density. There is no zero padding, frequency interpolation, or automatic DC removal. The FFT uses the exact samples shown in the export.

All computation stays in your browser. Listening is optional and plays one short, attenuated preview with brief fades; it does not change the analysis.

Samples and audio
